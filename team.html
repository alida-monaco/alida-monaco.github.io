The people

Founder, collaborators, and thought-partners.

Values As Vision is at an early, idea-stage moment. Here is who is shaping it, and in what capacity.

AM

Alida Monaco

Founder and principal operator

Alida is a dual-degree candidate pursuing a master’s in public policy at the Harvard Kennedy School and an MBA at MIT Sloan. She holds a B.A. in Environmental Science and Public Policy from Harvard College, won the Enel Endowment Prize for best undergraduate thesis in environmental economics, and published her senior thesis in the Journal of Sustainable Finance & Investment.

Before graduate school she was a Carbon Data Advisor at Watershed, advising companies with revenues over $1 billion, including Paramount, Wayfair, and Sweetgreen, on decarbonization planning. Earlier, as a Climate Change and Sustainability Analyst at ICF, she worked with local governments and federal agencies (EPA, USDA) on climate regulation. She has also held climate internships at the Chicago Department of Environment, the World Resources Institute, and the United Nations.

ML

Mia Lei

Co-organizer, Harvard Kennedy School pilot · not formally on the Values As Vision team

Mia is Co-Founder and Executive Director of CareForAll, a U.S. social movement organizing for health insurance that people can count on. She previously served as a Global Health Corps Fellow with the Clinton Health Access Initiative and holds a degree from the UNC Gillings School of Global Public Health. She is affiliated with Harvard University, where she is co-organizing the “Bridging Forward” pilot alongside Alida.

MJ

Myer Johnson-Potter

Thought-partner · not formally on the Values As Vision team

Myer is a Capital Mobilization Associate at the Glasgow Financial Alliance for Net Zero. He served as a Portfolio Risk Advisor in the U.S. Department of Energy’s Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations during the Biden Administration, and previously worked at McKinsey & Company. He graduated from Harvard College with a concentration in Environmental Science and Public Policy and a secondary in Government, and wrote his senior thesis on legislative pathways for U.S. decarbonization.
