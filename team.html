The people

Co-founders, collaborators, and thought-partners.

Conversations for Change is at an early, idea-stage moment. Here is who is shaping it, and in what capacity.

Alida Monaco

Co-founder

Alida is a joint MPP/MBA candidate at the Harvard Kennedy School and MIT Sloan, with a B.A. in Environmental Science and Public Policy from Harvard College. Her senior thesis, advised by economist James Stock, studied the effect of university fossil-fuel divestment on greenhouse gas emissions; it won the Enel Endowment Prize for best undergraduate thesis in environmental economics (co-recipient) and was published in the Journal of Sustainable Finance & Investment.

Before graduate school, Alida was a Carbon Data Advisor at Watershed, helping companies with revenues over $1 billion, including Paramount, Wayfair, and Sweetgreen, plan their decarbonization. Earlier, as a Climate Change and Sustainability Analyst at ICF, she worked with public- and private-sector clients and federal agencies, including the EPA, on emissions inventories, ESG disclosure, and climate regulation. She was a Summer Graduate Fellow at the Chicago Department of Environment in 2026 and has interned with the World Resources Institute and Harvard’s Office for Sustainability.

As a UN Millennium Fellow, she raised funds to install rain gardens on Harvard’s campus to reduce flooding: a local, tangible environmental problem solved through collaboration, the same spirit that drives this work. She co-organizes the “Bridging Forward” pilot at Harvard Kennedy School.

Abigail (Abby) Baisley

Co-founder

Abby is an MBA candidate at MIT Sloan (Class of 2028) on educational leave from McKinsey & Company, where she focused on sustainability, mining, and energy and is an incoming Summer Associate for 2027. Her experience centers on stakeholder relations and on how business strategy and procurement shape sustainability.

Most recently she was Program Manager of the Global Permanence Fund at ZOMALAB, advancing community economic development through conservation around the world. At McKinsey she worked as a Business Analyst in nature conservation finance, marketing, and mining, fields that showed her how interconnected communities, industries, and ecosystems are.

Abby holds a B.B.A. in Finance and Environmental Science from Emory University, where she helped build the Sustainable Business Group at the Goizueta Business School to help students explore sustainability careers.

ML

Mia Lei

Co-organizer, Harvard Kennedy School pilot · not formally on the Conversations for Change team

Mia is Co-Founder and Executive Director of CareForAll, a U.S. social movement organizing for health insurance that people can count on. She previously served as a Global Health Corps Fellow with the Clinton Health Access Initiative and holds a degree from the UNC Gillings School of Global Public Health. She is affiliated with Harvard University, where she is co-organizing the “Bridging Forward” pilot alongside Alida.

MJ

Myer Johnson-Potter

Thought-partner · not formally on the Conversations for Change team

Myer is a Capital Mobilization Associate at the Glasgow Financial Alliance for Net Zero. He served as a Portfolio Risk Advisor in the U.S. Department of Energy’s Office of Clean Energy Demonstrations during the Biden Administration, and previously worked at McKinsey & Company. He graduated from Harvard College with a concentration in Environmental Science and Public Policy and a secondary in Government, and wrote his senior thesis on legislative pathways for U.S. decarbonization.
